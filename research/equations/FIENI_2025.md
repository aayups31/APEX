# FIENI_2025 equation and parameter review

This is an **adaptation** of the supplied paper, not reproduction of its MINLP or
SAC policy. The [machine-readable map](fieni-2025-v1.json) covers all printed
equation numbers 1-70, symbols, units, code locations, matches, deviations and
omissions. Review date: 2026-10-08. Paper SHA-256:
fc609aa9dcff9179d5e6cba8ce96eb7785f8db45daeef2fc6c4aad32ad133270.
Pages 3-10 were checked visually as well as through text extraction. The PDF visibly
has an empty equation number 11; the map records that anomaly rather than supplying
an inferred expression.

## Frozen state and action contract

At the beginning of lap k, states contain battery/fuel energy (MJ), mass (kg),
elapsed race time (s), compound-change boolean, dry compound, dimensionless wear
and previous-pit/outlap flag. APEX also carries completed-lap index and the last
completed lap time. Controls are positive fuel consumption (MJ/lap), signed stored
battery delta (negative deployment, positive recharge) and optional pit compound.
The tyre used to price the current lap is the pre-action tyre: a pit changes the
next compound and resets next wear. Four nominal modes are selected by current pit
and previous pit. No pit occupancy is inferred from these research controls.

The paper uses an integer compound-change count. APEX retains a boolean: it has the
same at-least-one-change feasibility predicate, but different numerical state.
At the R012-R014 review, tyre age equations 23/24, polynomial MINLP auxiliaries,
fitted nonlinear lap maps, SAC training, forced final-20-lap compound changes and
published benchmarks were not implemented. Current battery energy affects feasible controls but does not
independently affect the synthetic nominal lap-time map, unlike the paper's maps.

Later R015-R017 additions have separate protocols: smooth synthetic maps,
bounded local IPOPT and [hybrid SAC training](../protocols/SAC_STRATEGY.md).
The frozen v1 machine-readable map remains the original core/environment review.
R017 uses a separate normalized wrapper, smooth objective and explicit pit masks;
its factorized policy and reward are declared adaptations. It does not reproduce
the paper's confidential maps, long-horizon benchmarks or published policy.

## Corrected mappings and feasibility

Equations 54-58 specify an affine normalized battery map with reversed endpoints.
With delta_min=-1.25 and delta_max=0.75, B=-1 gives 0.75 MJ recharge, B=0 gives
-0.25 MJ deployment and B=1 gives -1.25 MJ deployment. Zero physical delta occurs
at B=-0.25. The previous piecewise map incorrectly treated B=0 as neutral.

Let R=N-k-1 be the number of laps after the action and D=abs(delta_min).
The applied battery delta is projected into:

    [max(delta_min, -E_b),
     min(delta_max, capacity-E_b, R*D-E_b)]

The applied fuel consumption is projected into:

    [max(f_min, E_f-R*f_max), min(f_max, E_f-R*f_min)]

These intervals satisfy both present input/state bounds and a reachable zero-energy
finish. Empty intervals are errors, never permission to exceed per-lap limits.
States are checked for finite values, consistent fuel energy/mass, domains and
terminal reachability. Tiny floating-point interval crossings below 1e-9 are treated
as roundoff; this is not a physical-bound relaxation. Corrections retain requested
and applied actions plus named reasons.

The supplied Eq. 59 uses positive delta_b,max as the remaining depletion capacity.
Under its Eq. 1/4 sign convention, depletion capacity instead uses the magnitude
of negative delta_b,min. APEX declares this correction and evaluates reachable
sets for laps after the current action. It does not copy that printed bound or
silently reinterpret the signs. Terminal legality additionally checks valid state
and an exactly completed horizon; negative energy never passes as zero.

## Parameter provenance and surrogate domain

Every default parameter and all tyre wear/time-loss coefficients are **PRIOR**,
chosen synthetic values. They are not learned, FIA rules, contemporary car specs,
paper-identified coefficients or values calibrated against public races. The map
declares every parameter's units and coefficient units.

Wear uses the paper's affine mass-dependent form with synthetic coefficients.
Both wear updates and cubic tyre-time evaluation saturate at wear 1.25, an APEX
surrogate assumption beyond the paper's discussed data support. TransitionInfo
records wear saturation. The clipped cubic is not globally twice differentiable;
it must not be fed to a gradient-based solver as a smooth paper reproduction.
The environment adds a synthetic -1000 terminal illegality penalty; the paper's
unmodified offset-minus-lap reward is not claimed.

## Hand fixtures and exactness scope

[Five hand-calculated fixtures](../fixtures/fieni-hand-transition-v1.json) cover
normal, inlap, outlap, consecutive pit and terminal projection. Expected values
are frozen decimal arithmetic, not generated by calling the engine. Numerical
tolerance is absolute 1e-9; additional tests cover state/input bounds, reset timing,
same-compound legality, asymmetric normalization and saturation reporting.

ExactStrategyOracle enumerates every requested finite-grid action sequence under
the checked projected transition. Projected aliases are counted separately.
It checks a candidate budget before transitions and supports at most eight laps.
Failure returns no partial optimum. Its optimum is **only for that synthetic
projected grid**, never a continuous optimum or the paper's MINLP solution.

The existing DiscreteStrategyOracle remains an approximate beam benchmark by
default. Coarse bucketing or finite width can lose optimality. Its exact mode
dominates only states with identical future-relevant lap/resource/mass/compound/
wear/outlap values; objective time is compared separately. No optimality claim is
made if width pruning occurred. It now fails when no legal terminal remains,
instead of returning an infeasible state as a feasible result.

## Acceptance and rollback

Run `apexsim strategy-foundation-demo --output <new-directory>` from the checkout.
Inspect hand-fixture errors, exhaustive candidate/feasible counts, beam exactness,
regret and terminal energy. Run tests and inspect the
[verification report](../../docs/verification/fieni-foundation-report.md).
No public training, tuning or held-out benchmark is involved.
R012-R014 completion does not promote the simulator globally to R1.

including its known feasibility and map errors; saved artifacts stay unchanged.
