# Protocol — TODD_2025 Tyre-Energy Forecasting

## Primary claim
Telemetry histories can forecast four tyre-energy targets, with explainability suitable for race-strategy support.

## Current implementation
Source-bound public proxy samples, sequence-safe native windows, matched linear/
ridge/XGBoost/GRU/LSTM/TFT training and frozen seen/unseen circuit evaluation.
Temporal perturbation/explanation scaffolding remains outside accepted public
faithfulness evidence.

R024's [frozen dataset plan](TYRE_ENERGY_DATASET.md) adds native car/position
motion proxies on five development races. Its [data card](../../docs/data/tyre-energy-proxy.md)
requires per-field truth labels, source-row lineage, native interval units and
uninterrupted per-driver sequences. Measured wheel targets remain UNKNOWN.
The legacy event-only window helper must not mix drivers or bridge rejected
samples in this dataset. R025's [matched benchmark](TYRE_FORECAST_BENCHMARK.md)
and R026's [frozen seen/unseen study](TRACK_GENERALIZATION.md) are complete;
proxy-target and calibration limitations remain explicit. Broader measured-energy
accuracy and explanation/counterfactual faithfulness are pending.

## Critical boundary
The paper used proprietary 0.1 s Mercedes telemetry and measured/derived tyre-energy targets. APEX cannot reproduce those targets from public data. Proxy tests are adaptations only.

## Experiments
- synthetic recovery test; - FastF1/OpenF1 proxy prediction; - seen/unseen track split; - XGBoost vs RNN/GRU/TFT; - per-wheel and track-section error; - feature perturbation faithfulness; - VSC counterfactual; - later game temperature/wear correlation.

## Acceptance
No event leakage, simple baselines included, per-wheel metrics, unseen-track report, explanation faithfulness and explicit target provenance.
