# R041 correction for observed sector traffic

Keep the sealed v24 cohort, the frozen nomination/practice ridge,
whole-race exclusion, forecast bounds and both crossover gates.
R041's earlier experiments used proximity or aggregate driving
features. This test supplies the mean signed time adjustment
associated with observed sector-level following and DRS.

Set the two adjustments to the race median contrasts from the three
2022 events in the sealed R050 sector study. The R041 cohort starts
in 2023. Using the older events keeps the traffic calibration separate
from every R041 test race and its outcomes. Retain the signs and use
the R050 definitions for the three supported gap and DRS states.

For each of the three existing history windows, inspect sectors two
and three of its three laps. Use the sealed v30 timing links and the
original DRS samples from the v24 telemetry inventory. Require the
same preceding car at each sector's boundaries. Both cars' timing
links must be known by the original forecast cutoff. Require R050's
known-code coverage, maximum clock gap and DRS-open duration. Use
observations only on the archived history. An unknown sector supplies
no correction or support.

For each window sum the supported sectors' time adjustments and
divide by three laps. Record its supported fraction out of six
sectors. Fit a candidate with the three shifts and three fractions
added to the baseline. Fit a support-only control with the three
fractions. Score every original case and publish both controls, the
candidate, and the results for the seven recurring failures.

Continue only if the candidate increases the overall race pass count,
recovers a recurring failure and improves mean absolute contrast error
over the support-only control. Full development qualification is a
prerequisite for opening fresh confirmation. The observational
corrections do not measure physical tyre state.
