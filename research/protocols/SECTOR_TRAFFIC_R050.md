# R050 sector following-pace confirmation

The registered full-lap comparison had insufficient support. Retain
its results. Restrict the follow-up to individually timed sectors,
since a lap with DRS often still has sectors driven with the wing
closed. Use 2023 for development, the same five 2024 races as a second
development panel, and three separately archived 2022 races (rounds
3, 5 and 17) as confirmation. These confirmation events were excluded
from the original R050 investigation. Check the permanent benchmark
guard and verify archive bytes in all partitions.

Use accurate green, undeleted nonpit laps with native start and
intermediate sector crossing timestamps. Require the native sector
span to agree with its recorded duration to within 0.15 seconds.
Require the same adjacent same-lap leader at both ends of the sector,
without screening candidates by observed time residual. Analyze
the original DRS samples over the measured sector. Require 90%
support from known DRS codes, holes no wider than one second, and
at least one observed open second to classify an open sector. The
closed stratum has zero measured open time.

Match within driver, race, tyre stint and sector. Use nearest tyre
age within five laps without replacement per comparison. Estimate the
following term from closed sectors starting up to 1 s behind versus
closed sectors starting 2–4 s behind. Estimate the DRS term from open
against closed sectors starting within 1 s and additionally require
the matched start gaps to differ by at most 0.35 s. Use only completed
sector durations in the two empirical contrasts. Publish all per-race
pair counts, age imbalance, median effects and observed race ranges.

Freeze each coefficient as the median of the development race
medians from 2023. Require at least 25 matched pairs per term in
each of the three panels. Qualification requires a positive
following term and a negative DRS term in development, and sign
replication in at least three of five 2024 races and two of three
2022 confirmation races. Compare each frozen term against a zero
effect on all corresponding observed confirmation contrasts using
absolute error, with equal race weights. Require improvement for
both terms.

This calibrates conditional sector-time adjustments. It uses
retrospective observations, measured DRS state and timing-line
neighbors as a proxy for traffic exposure. Physical aerodynamic
coefficients and 2026’s different rules require separate evidence.
