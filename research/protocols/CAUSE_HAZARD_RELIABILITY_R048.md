# R048 sporting censoring and reliability trend

Preserve the first registered study. Treat disqualifications as
administrative censoring through completed laps and exclude explicitly
formation-lap incidents from race-lap exposure. Map accident damage,
cooling system and power loss to their documented broad causes.
Continue to leave ambiguous damage, punctures and illness unresolved.

Use the corrected training and held-out inventories with the same
cause coverage, support, log loss and count calibration gates. Keep
the first-versus-later incident hazards. Estimate each training year's
mechanical laps-at-risk rate with one half-count in the numerator and
one in the denominator. Freeze the geometric annual rate ratio from
2022 to 2023, and project the mechanical rate from 2023 to each test
year. Apply that mechanical probability in both lap bands and make
the stay probability the complement of the two causes. Reject invalid
probabilities. Compare against the corrected homogeneous baseline.

This follow-up addresses the previously observed reliability shift.
The 2024–2025 results are reused period holdouts. The projections'
numerical parameters come exclusively from 2022–2023. Report both
years separately and preserve the limits of historical extrapolation.
